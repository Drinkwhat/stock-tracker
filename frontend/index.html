Stock Tracker

Price alerts for your watchlist

Get an email when a stock comes within reach of your target price. Prices are checked every weekday at 10:00, 16:30 and 18:30 (Rome time) and compared in euro.

Start your watchlist

Import the stocks you follow from a CSV file, or add them one at a time.

CSV format

Semicolon-separated, UTF-8, with the columns nazione, Nome, prezzo Target Long and note. Target prices are in euro; Italian decimals such as 16,3 are fine. An optional ticker column skips the automatic lookup. Rows without a long target price, or with a note, are skipped.
